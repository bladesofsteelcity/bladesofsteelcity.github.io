Blades of Steel City brings together operators, midstream, service companies, and suppliers for one thing you can't get in a boardroom — a shift on the ice with the people you do business with every day.

We founded Blades of Steel City in Pittsburgh in 2025 and named our first tournament after it. In February 2026 we took the same format to West Texas under the banner Twig & Puck. Same organization, same mission — two basins.

Every team entry, every sponsorship, and every dollar raised at the rink goes to work in the communities where our industry lives: the towns we drill in, drive through, hire from, and call home. It's a day of hockey with a full year of impact behind it.

Compete

Round-robin play across two sheets. Beer-league rules, real trophies, and bragging rights that last until next season.

Connect

Locker rooms and a post-game reception put customers, vendors, and crews shoulder-to-shoulder — no badge scanners required.

Contribute

Net proceeds are presented on ice to local nonprofit partners selected in each host community.

Game Day

Schedule of Events — Nov 12, 2026

Four teams, two sheets, one half day. Games run on the UPMC Rink and Rink 2 at Printscape Arena.

Game Format

20-minute single-period games. Each team plays three round-robin games plus one placement game — four games on the day. Round-robin games can end in a tie.

Rules of Play

Adult league rules apply. This is a charity tournament — play hard, play smart, everybody gets back to work on Monday.

Equipment

Full equipment is required and is not optional: helmet, gloves, shoulder and elbow pads, shin guards, hockey pants, skates, and stick. Jerseys are provided at check-in.

Who Can Play

Current or past ice hockey experience is encouraged. This is not a learn-to-skate event. Players must be comfortable skating in a competitive game setting.

Standings Points

3Win
1Tie
0Loss

Round-robin points set the seeds for the placement games: No. 1 vs No. 2 for the championship, No. 3 vs No. 4 in the consolation game.

Tiebreakers

  1. Head-to-head result between the tied teams. Used for two-team ties only.
  2. Goal differential, capped at +5 per game — no reward for running up the score.
  3. Fewest goals allowed.
  4. Coin flip at the scorer’s table with both captains.

If three teams are tied, or the tied teams tied each other, skip straight to goal differential.

Medal Games

No ties. If a placement game is tied after regulation, it goes straight to a shootout — no overtime, to keep both sheets on schedule.

3 shooters per team, chosen by each bench. Still tied after three rounds: sudden death, one shooter at a time. No player shoots twice until every skater on the bench has gone.
